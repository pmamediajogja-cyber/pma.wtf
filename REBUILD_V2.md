# PMA.WTF — Rebuild V2

## Direction
PMA.WTF is evolving from a personal cyber portfolio into a digital hub under PT PMA Media Yogyakarta.

Primary goals:
- Instagram destination for free and paid design products
- Digital product storefront
- Design/service portfolio
- Personal profile and CV
- Articles / journal for SEO and monetization
- Adsterra monetization without compromising user experience

## Visual direction
- Dark editorial
- Subtle cyber / technology cues
- Premium and professional
- Minimal visual noise
- Lightweight, purposeful animation
- Mobile-first and performance-conscious

## Architecture principle
Keep React + Vite, but keep the architecture simple enough for the owner to understand and edit.

## Planned sections
1. Home
2. Free Designs
3. Premium Designs
4. Services
5. Articles
6. About / CV
7. PT PMA Media Yogyakarta
8. Contact

## Rebuild stages
- Stage 0: audit and simplify the existing React foundation
- Stage 1: visual system and homepage redesign
- Stage 2: design library and product data
- Stage 3: free / paid download flow
- Stage 4: articles
- Stage 5: profile, CV and company presence
- Stage 6: monetization and Adsterra
- Stage 7: SEO, analytics and Instagram landing flows
